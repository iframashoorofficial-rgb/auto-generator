# Landing page meme ideas

Written with the meme-engine skill on 21 Aug 2026. Thirty candidates were
generated, the linter killed four, and the rest were cut by hand. These six are
the survivors.

Audience: the business owner who currently makes their own posts.

---

## Netflix documentary ×2

Talking-head to camera, eyes just off-lens, chair, "On A Mission" by Duomo.
**Note:** the format was re-verified after these were written — real posts use a
single caption plus performance, not the four-slot chyron structure below. The
lines still work as a *script*, but shoot them as one confession to camera.

**The shoot**
- lower third — `the shoot, £600, 200 photos`
- ominous claim — `they used photo 3 and no one ever opened the folder`
- evidence label — `photos 4 to 200`
- witness line — `the folder is still there, i checked`

**The founder**
- lower third — `the founder, chief content officer`
- ominous claim — `he blocked 3 hours every friday and made 1 post`
- evidence label — `the calendar, still blocked`
- witness line — `friday was sacred, he said that a lot`

Both witness lines trail off rather than land. Don't cut to the product — end on
the witness.

## How different our lives are ×2

Split screen, same timestamp, strict grammatical parallel.
**Note:** this format is now marked unverified — the Hall & Oates track and the
timestamp slot could not be confirmed. Check a real example before filming.

**Idea drought** — `7am monday`
- me `googling 30 content ideas`
- her `binning 30 in 2 minutes`

The number 30 stays fixed; only the verb inverts.

**Onboarding** — `day 1`
- me `filling a 40 field brief`
- her `answering 6 questions once`

## Bob ×2 — ⚠️ do not film as written

These were built on a format definition that turned out to be wrong. The
registry described escalating unhinged reasons; the real trend is a list that
**refuses** to escalate — every reason is literally the same word ("1. Bob
2. Bob 3. Bob 4. Bob"). Kept here because the writing is reusable elsewhere, but
they are not this format.

**The agency** — `the £2k retainer renewed` → `they sent 4 quote posts` → `one had a typo` → `they used a competitor photo` → `invoice lands on the 1st`

**Dead grid** — `last post was 14 march` → `9 of 12 say hiring` → `3 handshake stock photos` → `caption ends in 30 hashtags` → `your mum stopped liking`

The escalation-to-dread shape suits **saxophone gets louder**, which is
genuinely about impending doom. Worth re-cutting them for that format.

## One that was cut

`1st of month` · me `paying £2k for 4 posts` / her `paying 0 for 40`.

The funniest of the ten and it passes the linter, but "0" reads as a pricing
claim and Format Studio needs an OpenRouter key. Only use it if you're happy to
defend the number.
